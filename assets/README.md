## Youth farming illustrations

`youth-milking.webp` and `youth-collection.webp` were generated for Maziwa Bila Hasara on 30 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. They show fictional adult young farmers in a Kenyan dairy context and are explicitly labelled **AI-created illustration** in English and Kiswahili. These two assets are separate from the licensed documentary photograph above. They are not documentary evidence or app-user testimonials. The final prompts are preserved in [YOUTH-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md](YOUTH-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md).

The three-slide gallery begins with youth milking, then collection preparation, then the original documentary photograph. Full scenes and captions are kept separate so controls and text do not obscure the farming activity.
